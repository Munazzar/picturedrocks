What this list is for

A trip keeps the countdown, the sun arc and the itinerary tab. A plain list drops all three and calls things done rather than packed — for groceries, chores, a move, anything. It only changes this room, so Pictured Rocks stays a trip.

Arranging

Hold an item or a category heading until it lifts, then move it. Items can cross into another category; categories only move within their own block, so pinned ones stay at the top. The pin sits at the right-hand end of every heading.
